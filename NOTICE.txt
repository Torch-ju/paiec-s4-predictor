source provenance is recorded in provenance/source_origins.json. Provenance is

or derived artifacts. A human rights holder and a project license remain to be
confirmed before public release.

provenance/ATTRIBUTION_SOURCE_REVIEW.txt. Its archive references identify the
historical review object; it is not a claim that all those archives are included
in this repository. Third-party dependencies are imported, not vendored. Their
own licenses apply. Obtain training data under the actual upstream conditions.
